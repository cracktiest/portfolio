Overview

Developed multiple collaborative robotic arm applications to automate repetitive material handling and production processes within garment manufacturing. The robotic systems were integrated into existing production lines to improve productivity, reduce operator workload, and ensure consistent process quality while maintaining safe human-robot collaboration.

Challenge

  • Several garment production processes relied heavily on manual operations, including transferring garment inner boxes, positioning machine templates, and performing repetitive steam ironing for cohesive attachment.
  • These tasks required high precision, repetitive movements, and continuous operator involvement, leading to fatigue, inconsistent quality, and limited production efficiency.

Solution

Custom Robotic Workstations

Designed and implemented collaborative robotic arm solutions tailored for multiple production applications.

Automatic Inner Box Handling

Automated the transfer of garment inner boxes between production stations using robotic motion and custom end-effectors.

Template Sheet Handling

Implemented automatic loading and unloading of acrylic (mica) templates for sewing and production machines.

Steam Iron Automation

Automated steam ironing operations during cohesive attachment to ensure consistent pressure, positioning, and processing time.

Industrial Integration

Integrated programmable robot motion, digital I/O communication, pneumatic devices, and customized mechanical fixtures to achieve reliable industrial operation.

Result

  • Successfully deployed collaborative robotic arm applications across multiple garment production processes.
  • Reduced manual handling and improved process consistency.
  • Increased operational efficiency while maintaining safe human-robot collaboration.
