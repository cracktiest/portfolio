Overview

Supported the deployment, maintenance, and continuous operation of AMRs used for material handling and internal logistics in a garment manufacturing facility. This included robot commissioning, factory mapping, navigation optimization, troubleshooting, and operational support to ensure reliable autonomous transportation across production areas.

Challenge

  • Reliable navigation and stable robot performance were essential as AMRs became part of daily operations.
  • Frequent production layout changes required continuous map updates and calibration.
  • Preventive maintenance was needed to reduce downtime and maintain productivity.

Solution

Deployment & Commissioning

Performed robot deployment and commissioning by creating navigation maps, configuring operating environments, and optimizing travel routes.

Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Conducted preventive maintenance and diagnosed hardware and software issues to keep robots operating reliably.

Operational Support

Supported production teams to ensure uninterrupted robot operation while the fleet expanded across multiple departments.

Result

  • Successfully supported the operation and maintenance of the factory’s AMR fleet.
  • Performed factory mapping, navigation calibration, and route optimization.
  • Reduced operational downtime through preventive maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • Supported fleet expansion from 5 robots during the initial deployment phase to more than 15 robots within two years.
  • Gained hands-on experience with service AMRs, industrial transport AMRs, and autonomous lifting AMRs.

Fleet Expansion

  • Service AMRs: 10
  • Industrial Transport AMRs: 5
  • Autonomous Lifting AMRs: 5

Conclusion

This project strengthened my practical expertise in industrial robotics by providing hands-on experience with deployment, operation, and maintenance of autonomous mobile robots in a real manufacturing environment. Supporting fleet growth and maintaining reliable day-to-day operations improved my understanding of robot navigation, fleet management, industrial logistics, and factory automation.
