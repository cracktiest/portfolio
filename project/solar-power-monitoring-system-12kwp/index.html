Overview

Developed and deployed a 1.2 kWp solar photovoltaic (PV) system to support renewable energy adoption within the manufacturing facility. The project included solar panel installation, inverter integration, electrical distribution, and a real-time monitoring platform that enables engineers to monitor energy generation, battery status, and inverter performance remotely using the Solar2MQTT system.

Challenge

  • The company required a reliable renewable energy solution to reduce dependence on grid electricity while providing continuous visibility into solar energy production and system performance.
  • Manual monitoring of inverter parameters made it difficult to evaluate system efficiency, detect abnormal conditions, and analyze long-term energy generation.

Solution

PV System Installation

Designed and installed a 1.2 kWp solar power system consisting of photovoltaic panels, an inverter, battery storage, and protection devices.

Solar2MQTT Integration

Integrated the inverter with Solar2MQTT running on an ESP8266 microcontroller for real-time data acquisition.

Remote Monitoring

Published operational data over MQTT for monitoring of solar panel voltage, battery status, AC output, grid status, temperature, and energy performance.

Scalable Dashboard Integration

Enabled integration with Node-RED, Home Assistant, and other Industrial IoT platforms for centralized energy management.

Result

  • Successfully installed and commissioned a 1.2 kWp solar energy system with continuous real-time monitoring.
  • Improved system visibility and simplified maintenance activities.
  • Enabled remote supervision of renewable energy generation and performance analysis.
